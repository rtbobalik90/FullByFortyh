LIES & TRUTH
"I've messed up too much. God is tired of me."
God disciplines sons; He doesn't discard them. Your repeated returning may be evidence of grace, not rejection.
"My addiction tells me who I really am."
Struggle reveals wounds; it does not define identity. A man fighting sin is different than a man surrendering to it.
"I ruined my family and therefore ruined my purpose."
Failure changes stories; it does not cancel calling.
"I'm unworthy of love, intimacy, or peace."
Worth was assigned by Christ before performance.
"My daughters would be better with a different father."
Your daughters do not need a perfect man. They need their dad — present, repentant, playful, growing, loving Jesus.
"I have to carry everything alone."
Strength and isolation are not the same thing.
"I'm behind everyone else."
Growth at 36 is still growth. Many men never start.
